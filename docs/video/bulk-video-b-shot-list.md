# Bulk promo, version B: shot list (1:22, 1080p30, no voiceover)

**Intro to outro.** It opens on a centred cover on paper, with the kicker "UCL Claude Hackathon · Better Student Life", the logo and "BULK", and "Order together. / Unlock the deal. / Share one delivery." with the middle line in lime-ink, then the subline "Group food ordering for students". A full-bleed lime card slides up with the why: "£15.93 for a £12 takeaway", with the subline "Fees add about a third. 63% of students skip meals to save money." When it slides away, the real app's phone springs up, and a pale lime disc fades in behind it. The disc only appears with the phone and only changes sides when the phone does.

**Joining.** The phone keeps moving and zooms in on every tap, and each tap gets a ring and a burst. Each step has a numbered caption pill and a huge headline whose key word lands on a lime highlighter that wipes in. A caption on its own sits vertically centred. When a stat card arrives, the caption glides up so the pair is centred together. Spring-popped stickers mark the payoffs:
- 01 "Parties near you".
- 02 "Join Burger Yard": a close-up of the "£7 left, 15% off" highlight.
- 03 "Pick your meal": a close-up of "Add Yard Classic", then Cola.
- 04: the phone glides left, and the basket zooms in. The headline is "You save 23%", a red "−23%" sticker sits on the breakdown, and a stat card reads "£11.67 instead of £15.24 ordering alone".
- 05 "Tap to join".
- 06 "Deal reached", with a "15% off!" sticker on the "Party filled!" popup.
- 07 "Pay your share": a close-up of "Pay £11.67".
- 08 "Your bag. Your PIN.": a close-up of the pickup PIN with an "Only yours" sticker. The delivery wait is compressed to about a second.
- 09 "Show your PIN": the courier handover code, then "Code accepted".

**Hosting.**
- 10 "No party? Not the right food? No worries." on the home screen, then "Start your own": a close-up of the + button, then a zoom out to the whole create screen, then close-ups of Kumo Ramen, "Add Tonkotsu Ramen" and the gyoza, the "Create party" bubble, and "Create party".
- 11 "Your party's live": zoomed out on the whole lobby, with the countdown and "55% filled".
- 12 "Invite friends": Chloe and Maya. The phone zooms back out to the whole lobby, and a stat card reads "£20 for Deliveroo's 30% off. One £12 basket never gets there. A party does."
- 13 "Deal reached": the "Party filled!" popup with a "+2 friends" sticker, and "Someone drops out? Nobody's charged."

**Close.** A paper outro reads "Download Bulk now" with the tagline, which gives way to the Bulk logo lockup on paper as the closing shot.

The app demo plays at 1.5×, fast enough to hold attention but slow enough to follow each step. All motion is transform and opacity only. Entrances use `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`. Glides and zooms use `cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)`. The phone pop, stickers, highlighted words and stat cards use springs (bounce 0.18–0.3), and text staggers at 70ms.
